# Anima Tua

You lost your memories and are trapped in your mind. While adventuring through a dungeon, filled with monsters, you step by step uncover your fate.

## Our opinion on AI

AI can be helpful with optimizing the coding workflow. It can easyly detect bugs and suggest fixes. But it can't be creative. For this porject the use of AI for any creative task was and will be stricly prohibited.
